## Q1. Product catalogue: what each does in the find → remix → produce chain, target user, price, platforms, data source, traction, handles

### Takeaway
About 22 products map onto four layers. Discovery+script tools (Sandcastles, Virlo, Blort, Spotter, vidIQ Remix, Format Finder) stop at a script or brief and produce no video. "Clone this ad/format" generators (Creatify, MakeUGC, Higgsfield, Arcads, Icon) go from a reference ad to an AI-actor video, but the reference is mostly Meta/ad-library ads, not organic TikTok/Reels outliers. Editors/clippers (Opus, Submagic, Descript, Vizard, Klap, Choppity, Veed, Captions, CapCut, Munch) cut or caption footage you already have and do not find trends.

## Q3. What is commoditised vs rare? Where is the gap?

### Takeaway
Commoditised: outlier lists, auto-captions, long-to-short clipping, AI-actor UGC, URL-to-ad. Rare: converting an organic outlier into a format-level, human-shootable shot list for a different brand with client sign-off. That is the opening for Trend Finder, though well-funded generators are moving up the chain.

### Cited Findings
- Discovery+script tools cluster at $49 entry (Virlo $49, Sandcastles $49, Spotter $49, reported) with agency tiers $199–$499. — pages above.
- Clipping/captions tier is $14–$29 entry across Klap $14, Submagic $19, Choppity $24, Opus $15–29, Descript $16–24 annual-billed/monthly mixed. — pages above [checked original page].
- Generators: AI-UGC entry $15 (Creatify Standard) to $29 (MakeUGC) to ~$110 (Arcads, third-party). Rival pitch is that cloning removes the human shoot; Icon moved the other way (human UGC at $1,000/mo, "no AI"). — [Icon](https://www.icon.com), [Creatify](https://www.creatify.ai/pricing).
- Capital flowing to generation, not discovery: Higgsfield $5.4B valuation, HeyGen >$200M ARR, Mirage/Captions $75M, Creatify $15.5M Series A; no funding found for Sandcastles, Blort, Format Finder; Virlo funding not stated. — sources in Q1.
- Format-as-product appears in small tools: Format Finder (65+ named formats), Icon (18 curated formats), MakeUGC Content Library, Arcads presets. — Q1 sources.
- Meta-ads library is the dominant "competitor reference" source (Creatify, Virlo Pro, Icon), not TikTok organic. — Q1 sources.

### Inferences
- Gap A: organic-outlier → format cluster → brand-specific shot list (not an AI-actor video) with per-client approval. Gap B: tools that assume the editor/human shoots (agencies selling real footage) are served by none; Icon's pivot suggests demand for human shoots exists, but this is one data point.
- Risk: Higgsfield "Recreate" and Seedance-based pipelines (Virlo+Claude user workflows) lower the cost of skipping the human shoot; agencies selling approval + human craft need to differentiate on trust/brand safety.

### Gaps
- No usage or churn data for any category; "commoditised" is judged from pricing-page overlap only.

## Q4. Legal / ToS / ethical caveats about copying viral videos

### Takeaway
Commentators agree viral status grants no licence, platform terms license the platform (not other users), and music/commercial use are the sharpest risks; AI remix liability is unsettled. Sources are law-firm blogs and vendor content; none is legal advice.

### Cited Findings
- Creator keeps copyright; the platform gets a broad licence that does not extend to other users. — [Sprintlaw TikTok copyright](https://sprintlaw.co.uk/articles/tiktok-copyright-issues/), [VMatch consulting guide](https://vmatchconsulting.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-repurposing-tiktok-content-without-violating-copyright) [search snippet only; consultancy opinion].
- Commercial-use music: business accounts need the Commercial Music Library; trending sounds are non-commercial only. — same sources [search snippet only].

### Inferences
- Ideas, formats and structures are generally less protectable than footage, script text, music and likeness (general principle; not verified by a lawyer here). Cloning a specific creator's performance/likeness (Recreate, Motion Control, Ad Remake) is higher risk than abstracting a format.
- A product that stores the source video only as a reference link and outputs a new shot list for a human shoot sidesteps most of this exposure; worth saying in positioning, with counsel review.
