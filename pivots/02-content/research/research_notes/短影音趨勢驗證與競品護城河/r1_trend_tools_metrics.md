## Q1. Which metrics/signals define "trending", "outlier" or "viral" in each tool? (exact formulas / thresholds where published)

### Takeaway
Almost every outlier tool uses the same core idea (views ÷ the same account's typical views), but vendors disagree on the baseline (mean vs median, window size, matched video age) and almost none publish the exact formula. The only fully published formulas found are Subscribr (mean of up to 10 surrounding videos), Shortimize's blog (median in same time window), OverseerOS's benchmark (median of comparable, same-format, age-matched uploads with confidence levels by baseline size), Rival IQ (engagement ÷ followers; TikTok ÷ views) and Dash Social (0-10 score from two indicators against a 25,000-video benchmark, weights undisclosed). "Trending" for formats/sounds/hashtags is defined by adoption growth (count of accounts/posts using it over time), but no vendor publishes a threshold; our "3-4 accounts = Early, 5+ = Rising" rule is more explicit than anything found.

### Cited Findings

#### YouTube-centric outlier tools (long-form + Shorts)

- vidIQ Outliers: "The outlier score measures a video's performance relative to the average performance of other videos on the same channel", compared against "the channel's average views within a similar timeframe." No explicit formula is given; examples: 10,000 views on a channel averaging 1,000 = 10x; colour bands black <2x, blue 2-5x, purple 5-10x, red >10x (help page itself says "three colors" but lists four and bands overlap at 5x). Filters include Outlier Score 0-100+, Views, Subscribers, Views Per Hour (0-1K+), Video Length, Publish Date. Advanced filters are paid-only. [VENDOR-DOC] — [vidIQ Help: Outliers](https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/9660010-outliers)
- vidIQ blog (published 2025-11-24) defines Outlier Score as "how far a video exceeds the channel's average performance" and Views Per Hour (VPH) as "how fast the video is gaining traction"; Shorts can be selected as a video type in filters. [VENDOR-MKT] — [vidIQ blog: Grow Faster on YouTube with the vidIQ Outliers Tool](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/vidiq-outliers-tool/)
- Third-party sites say vidIQ's baseline is a 30-90 day channel average, but vidIQ's own page does not confirm the window. [3P] — [search summary citing Creator Signal / Youviral](https://creatorsignal-site.pages.dev/blog/outlier-method-youtube-video-ideas/) (not verified against vidIQ).
- OutlierKit: score is "a performance multiplier that shows how much a YouTube video exceeded its channel's average views", "Calculated by comparing video views to the channel's average performance over recent uploads." Mean vs median and number of uploads not specified; page undated (footer © 2026). Its own guidance: 2x "might be noise", 3x+ significant, 5x+ "potential viral hit", suggested research focus 3x-10x, 20x+ often "involve external factors". It states the score "shows that a video outperformed, not why." [VENDOR-DOC] — [OutlierKit: What Is a YouTube Outlier Score?](https://outlierkit.com/resources/outlier-scores/)
- 1of10: outlier score is "a simple ratio: a video's views divided by its channel's typical views"; baseline calculation (which videos, window) not disclosed; no minimum cutoff, describes outliers as 10x-100x (review dated 2026-07-31, written by 1of10's team). Claims it "scans millions of channels". Has views-per-hour tracking per OutlierKit's comparison. [VENDOR-MKT] — [1of10 Review (1of10.com blog)](https://1of10.com/blog/1of10-review/); VPH claim from [COMPETITOR] [OutlierKit: 1of10 vs Viewstats](https://outlierkit.com/resources/1of10-vs-viewstats/)
- Viewstats: has "Outlier Score" and "Outlier Videos" in its dashboard and an in-YouTube overlay/Chrome extension (per OutlierKit's comparison). I found NO Viewstats-authored formula (its Zendesk help article returned HTTP 403). One search summary attributed a "previous ten videos" baseline to a third-party blog, but the one page I fetched that was suspected to say this (TubeAnalytics) does not contain it — treat as UNVERIFIED. [COMPETITOR] — [OutlierKit: 1of10 vs Viewstats](https://outlierkit.com/resources/1of10-vs-viewstats/); [TubeAnalytics: Viewstats vs TubeAnalytics](https://tubeanalytics.net/blog/viewstats-vs-tubeanalytics-outlier-discovery) (checked; no formula).
- Subscribr (YouTube script tool, useful as the only explicit published formula): "The outlier score is the ratio of the video's views to this average (rounded to 1 decimal place)" where the average is the mean view count of "a window of up to 10 surrounding videos (published before and after)" the video. Example: 20,000 vs 10,000 avg = 2.0. No thresholds. Also a separate "Velocity Score" ("a channel's current momentum and growth potential": outperforming usual content, consistent publishing, engagement relative to subscribers, multiple breakout videos in last month, accelerating viewership) — formula not given. [VENDOR-DOC] — [Subscribr Help: What are Velocity and Outlier Scores?](https://help.subscribr.ai/article/48-what-are-velocity-and-outlier-scores)
- TubeBuddy Content Outliers ("a new TubeBuddy Labs tool", page undated, images from 2026/09): outlier = "a video that dramatically outperforms other videos in a specific channel", baseline = "what a channel's videos usually do (average views)"; no formula or cutoff ("4-5X" used only as an illustration); results are "scaled to your channel size" (method unexplained). [VENDOR-MKT] — [TubeBuddy: Outlier YouTube videos and how to find them](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/outlier-youtube-videos-and-how-to-find-them/)
- TubeBuddy Keyword Explorer score (not an outlier metric, but shows disclosure level): based on search volume and competition; weighted (paid) vs unweighted; no formula or weights published. [VENDOR-DOC] — [TubeBuddy Keyword Explorer scores explained](https://tubebuddy.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/5000846613-tubebuddy-keyword-explorer-scores-explained)

#### Short-form multi-platform tools (TikTok / Instagram / Shorts)

- CILA (cila.app; "Competitor Intelligence Listening Analytics"; Instagram + TikTok only): "CILA compares each post against that account's usual performance" to produce an outlier score; sortable/filterable by content type, platform, account type, paid-partnership flag, time period. Shows trending sounds with number of posts using them. Metrics listed: outlier score, engagement rate, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, followers at posting time. Formula and baseline (mean/median/window) not disclosed. [VENDOR-DOC/landing] — [CILA](https://cila.app)
- Shortimize (vendor blog dated 2026-01-20): recommends "Outlier Score = Video Views ÷ Account Median Views (in same time window)" (example: 140,000 ÷ 20,000 median = 7x); says product "explicitly supports median and virality metrics and outlier identification at the account level"; "viral" is "subjective", often ~1M views quickly. Its "Pattern Confidence = (Repeatability × Cross-Creator × Recency × Clarity) − Saturation" (each 1-5) is the blog's own heuristic, NOT a documented product formula. Minimum fields to log per video: platform, account, post time, views, likes, comments, shares/saves, caption, length, URL. [VENDOR-MKT] — [Shortimize: How to Find Viral Video Patterns in Your Niche (2026 Guide)](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-find-viral-video-patterns-in-your-niche)
- Shortimize is an account tracker: it "tracks accounts you specify" and "doesn't surface content you don't already know about", with "No hashtag, sound, or trend intelligence" (per rival Virlo; competitor tracking "coming soon" per same). [COMPETITOR] — [Virlo: Shortimize alternatives](https://virlo.ai/blog/shortimize-alternatives)
- Virlo (2025-26 entrant; TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels): defines trend by adoption — "A trend is a format, sound or topic that more accounts are using each day inside a defined audience"; a single high-reach video is an outlier. Signals: adoption over time vs niche baseline, rate of change; "rising usage plus rising median performance indicates a live trend. Rising usage plus falling performance indicates saturation"; "early" = "before saturation, which on short-form usually means days rather than weeks". No formulas or numeric thresholds published. [VENDOR-MKT] — [Virlo Learning: Trends and trend detection](https://virlo.ai/learning/trends)
- Film & Flow "Short-Form Outliers" newsletter: "The 'Outlier Score' is a measure of how many times more views this got vs. the creator's usual posts" (baseline not stated; example 7872x). [3P/newsletter] — [Film & Flow](https://filmandflow.beehiiv.com/p/overthinking-your-content-again)
- OverseerOS benchmark report (vendor; published 2026-07-28, updated 2026-08-20; YouTube-focused but the most rigorous public methodology found): "Outlier score = Video views ÷ Comparable channel baseline views"; preferred baseline = median of recent comparable uploads from the same channel, same format, matched age, focal video excluded; average as secondary check; baseline size confidence: <5 "Very low", 10-19 "Moderate", 20-30 "High"; age snapshots 24h / 7d / 28d / 90d. Classes: <1x below baseline; 1-1.49x normal; 1.5-1.99x notable; 2-2.99x emerging; 3-4.99x strong; 5-9.99x breakout; 10-19.99x exceptional; 20x+ "extreme (audit the baseline)". Says subscriber-based denominators are weak, Shorts and long-form need separate baselines, no credible universal outlier rate across niches, and ">20x may reflect a fragile denominator". It publishes no sample size for the thresholds. [VENDOR-MKT, but transparent] — [OverseerOS: YouTube Outlier Benchmark Report 2026](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-outlier-benchmark-report)

### Inferences
- The baseline choices differ materially (mean of 10 surrounding videos at Subscribr; median in same window at Shortimize; median of age-matched same-format uploads at OverseerOS; "average" at vidIQ/OutlierKit/TubeBuddy), so "7x" is not comparable across tools. A believable product should publish its baseline, window, minimum sample (OverseerOS suggests ≥10 comparable posts for "Moderate" confidence), and exclude the focal video. Our current "median views, fallback views ÷ followers" is in the median camp; OverseerOS explicitly calls subscriber/follower denominators weak.
- vidIQ, 1of10, Viewstats expose views-per-hour (velocity) as a separate filter next to outlier score; no short-form tool found publishes a velocity formula. Outlier (relative) + velocity (time-normalised) + format adoption (breadth) are the three recurring signal families.
- Virlo's "rising usage + rising median performance = live trend; rising usage + falling performance = saturation" is the clearest published conceptual rule for the Early/Rising/Saturated stages; our Early/Rising stages (3-4 vs 5+ accounts in 14 days) are explicit but have no published peer equivalent to validate thresholds. This is an inference: no vendor publishes a count threshold.
- No tool found publishes statistical significance, confidence intervals or false-positive rates for "trending"; only OverseerOS gives confidence by baseline size. This is a differentiation opportunity for a "PostHog/Amplitude for video" claim.

### Gaps
- Exact Viewstats outlier formula: official help page blocked (HTTP 403); no verified source. 
- Exact vidIQ, 1of10, OutlierKit baseline (mean/median/window): not published.
- TikTok Creative Center's actual rank / "Breakout" formula: not published in official help found; only third-party descriptions.
- Pentos, Trendpop, Exolyt (Exo Score), Dash Social (weights) and Tubular: no formula-level methodology found. Pentos official site would not load.
- Sprout Social "trending TikTok posts" alert logic: only seen in a search summary, not fetched.
- Rival IQ 2025/2026 benchmark primary reports not fetched; sample sizes unverified.

## Q2. How does each tool explain WHY a video performed? (hook analysis, format labels, transcripts, AI summaries, benchmark comparisons)

### Takeaway
Explanation is the weakest, least-evidenced layer. Tools that explicitly document it do so by (a) exposing the post's hook/topic/format/transcript for manual study (CILA), (b) a vendor-generated "why it worked" text with unexplained method (TubeBuddy), or (c) benchmark comparison scores (Dash Social). Several vendors state on the record that public data cannot explain causation (OutlierKit, OverseerOS). Claims of AI "hook analysis" for short-form mostly come from small standalone tools, not the established platforms.

### Cited Findings
- CILA: "You can study each winning post's hook, topic, format, and performance, and read its transcript"; outlier scores draw on "public performance signals such as views, engagement, relative performance and content format patterns." Method for classifying hook/format not disclosed. Shows trending sounds with count of posts using them. [VENDOR-DOC/landing] — [CILA](https://cila.app)
- TubeBuddy Content Outliers: "tells you why the break-out videos worked" with "a stated reason it worked" and a starting concept; names possible drivers "the topic, the keyword, the format"; how reasons are produced/verified is not explained. [VENDOR-MKT] — [TubeBuddy blog](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/outlier-youtube-videos-and-how-to-find-them/)
- OutlierKit says plainly: "The score shows that a video outperformed, not why. The page says you must analyze the title, thumbnail, and hook yourself." [VENDOR-DOC] — [OutlierKit: outlier scores](https://outlierkit.com/resources/outlier-scores/)
- OverseerOS: "Public data discovers the anomaly. Private analytics diagnoses your own result" — CTR, retention, impressions and revenue are not visible in public data. [VENDOR-MKT] — [OverseerOS report](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-outlier-benchmark-report)
- vidIQ Outliers page shows engagement, subscriber count and average views per video; the Nov 2025 blog does not describe an AI "why it performed" feature (its Remix tool generates ideas, scripts, titles, thumbnails from outlier data). [VENDOR-DOC/MKT] — [vidIQ help](https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/9660010-outliers); [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/vidiq-outliers-tool/)
- Virlo: Content Studio "generates briefs directly from trending data"; its page does not describe hook/transcript/structural analysis. [COMPETITOR-authored comparison of Virlo itself, so VENDOR-MKT] — [Virlo: Shortimize alternatives](https://virlo.ai/blog/shortimize-alternatives)
- Shortimize: AI search "by context or hook" and "optimal lengths, virality/outlier views, and similar accounts/videos" in analyzers; no causal explanation described. [VENDOR-MKT] — [Shortimize blog](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-find-viral-video-patterns-in-your-niche)
- Dash Social: explains performance only as a score vs an internal benchmark (engagement + retention for TikTok; engagement + reach for Reels), by video length, scores revised as industry averages shift. [VENDOR-DOC] — [Dash Social glossary](https://www.dashsocial.com/digital-marketing-dictionary/entertainment-score)
- Dash Hudson Vision (2022 press release, OLD): "predictive, AI-based" visual analysis of what competitor visuals will perform best on a brand's channel; no methodology published. [3P/press] — [Newswire.ca press release](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/dash-hudson-s-ai-powered-competitive-solution-enables-brands-to-outperform-the-competition-on-social-media-847945834.html) (seen via search summary only).
- Exolyt Advanced tier lists sentiment analysis and video transcriptions as features; no hook analysis described. [VENDOR-DOC] — [Exolyt pricing](https://exolyt.com/pricing)
- Small standalone AI tools (Reelyze, Socialrails viral analysis tool, TransClipper) advertise frame-by-frame hook, pacing, on-screen text and drop-off analysis, "viral potential scores", replication templates — vendor claims, unverified. [3P/SEARCH-SNIPPET] — [Socialrails: Viral Reels & TikTok Analysis Tool](https://socialrails.com/free-tools/viral-reels-analysis-tool)

### Inferences
- The credible, evidenced explanation pattern across vendors is: show the outlier ratio against a stated baseline, then present observable features (hook text/first seconds, format label, sound, length, posting time, transcript) and compare them with the account's and niche's other posts, while avoiding causal language. The vendors that are candid (OutlierKit, OverseerOS) say causation cannot be shown from public data; a recommendation engine claiming "why" should phrase it as "features shared by N outliers vs M non-outliers".
- Cross-creator repetition is the only anti-fluke device any vendor proposes (OutlierKit: patterns across several outliers beat a single high score; Shortimize "Pattern Confidence" factors Cross-Creator and Repeatability), which maps directly to our "Early = 3-4 accounts" logic.

### Gaps
- No primary documentation found of how CILA, TubeBuddy or Shortimize generate hook/format labels or "reasons" (LLM vs rules), nor accuracy evaluation.
- Viewstats' explanation features not verified (help pages blocked).
- Whether any vendor validates its "why" explanations against holdout data: nothing found.

## Q3. Data sources (official API vs scraping), freshness, disclosed sample sizes/methodology

### Takeaway
Data collection methods are rarely disclosed. Where stated, short-form tools rely on public content (scheduled scraping or account tracking) rather than owner-authorised APIs, and refresh ranges from 24h defaults to 6x/day on top tiers. Sample sizes/methodology are disclosed almost only for benchmark scores (Dash Social 25,000 videos; Rival IQ medians without N) and not for trend/outlier detection.

### Cited Findings
- CILA: "only analyzes publicly available content", no passwords or connected accounts, any public creator/brand can be tracked without owner permission; tracked accounts refresh daily (Creator plan) or every 6 hours (Business plan). [VENDOR-DOC] — [CILA](https://cila.app)
- Virlo: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels via "scheduled scraping"; Meta Ads Intelligence on Pro+; Starter has "daily data refresh"; "Hashtag rankings across 500K+ tags" (marketing claim). [COMPETITOR-authored/VENDOR-MKT] — [Virlo: Shortimize alternatives](https://virlo.ai/blog/shortimize-alternatives); [Virlo Learning](https://virlo.ai/learning/trends)
- Shortimize: default refresh 24h; 1-hour and 4-hour refresh are paid add-ons (per rival Virlo); platforms TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, X; collection method (API vs scraping) not stated by Shortimize. [COMPETITOR + VENDOR-MKT] — [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/shortimize-alternatives); [Shortimize blog](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-find-viral-video-patterns-in-your-niche)
- Exolyt: "not affiliated with TikTok", "continuously updated"; official-API use not stated. Data update frequency by plan: 1x/day (Basic), 2x/day (Essentials), 6x/day (Advanced); chart history 7 days / 90 days / at least 1 year. [VENDOR-DOC] — [Exolyt guide](https://exolyt.com/guides/Exolyt-for-TikTok-analytics); [Exolyt pricing](https://exolyt.com/pricing)

- Tubular Labs: ML + observational panel "tracks anonymized social video viewership behaviors from millions of devices"; ~600 data-science experiments claimed; full methodology not public. ### Inferences
