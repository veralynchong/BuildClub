Current flow, as built

The app has no URLs. One React state value (view) picks the screen, a second (workspace) is either all or a client id. Two paths sit outside the shell: /method and the approver's /review/<token> page. The demo always signs you in as a team member.

Dead ends and gaps in today's flow

  1. The demo can never approve. "Try the demo" always signs in as a team member, and there is no role switch in the app. The approval step, the product's key moment, is only reachable by signing out and choosing "Manager (approves)". LoginScreen.tsx tryDemo · App.tsx
  2. All clients Home speaks to the manager, whoever is signed in. As a team member it says "1 more is waiting for you to approve", and Open leads to a read-only Review. OverviewScreen.tsx (seen live as Team member)
  3. Canvas Back always goes to the plan. Opened from the sidebar Canvas or from Plans "Make the video", Back still lands on the Plan page, not where you came from. App.tsx CanvasScreen onBack
  4. Two ways to make a plan, with different rules. Research says plans are made by setting a row to ★ Picked; Home's "Plan the shots" makes one directly, and Results only shows a plan button once a plan exists. App.tsx planNow, planFromVideo, pickRow
  5. Finished videos can't be approved in the app. "Send to manager for approval" on a cut only reaches the approver link page. The in-app Review shows plans only, and the link is made only on the Plans board's Waiting column. CutPanel, ExportBar, ReviewScreen, ShortlistBoard
  6. Making the video lives in three places. Plan page bottom (checklist, The video, Post), Canvas, and the editor inside Canvas. The sidebar Canvas is gated on approval and needs a picker. PlanScreen, CanvasPick, CanvasScreen
  7. Compare videos and Channel are dead ends. Their results can't be saved to Research or turned into a plan, and on All clients they have no client to save to. CompareScreen, ChannelScreen
  8. Brief is a leaf. Home passes an "edit brief" handler that is never rendered, and nothing links to Brief or its client memory except the sidebar. Memory is shown, not used. ClientHome onEditBrief unused
  9. Client side is a stub. "Continue" as Client ends at "coming soon"; ClientPortal.tsx is built but not wired. LoginScreen, ClientPortal
  10. No addresses. Browser Back leaves the app, nothing is linkable, and Results, Review, Plan, Board and Canvas don't survive a refresh (they fall back to Find ideas or Plans). App.tsx restoreTab
  11. "Sent back" has no column. The Plans badge counts sent-back plans, but they sit in Still planning with no lane of their own. ShortlistBoard COLUMNS
  12. Layout at about 1050px wide. Find ideas' "Your searches" table squeezes into a one-letter-wide column, and the Plans board's third column is cut off (it scrolls sideways). seen live, localhost:5192
  13. Leftover props. onFindMore on the Plans board is unused. The landing in-app (logo) has no "Back to the app", only Log in and Try the demo, which both just return. ShortlistBoard, App.tsx showLanding
Flow 1 · linear pipeline

Stage Rail

A client is a six-step pipeline shown as a numbered rail: Find, Collect, Plan, Approve, Make, Post. Every stage page ends with one "Next" button, and the rail shows how many items sit at each stage. The app teaches the job by its shape.

Principle

One direction, one primary action per page. Stage pages replace tabs. Plans stops being a board and becomes stage 3 and 4 lists. Production gets its own stage instead of sitting at the bottom of the plan.

Serves best

New team members, juniors and the demo. Anyone who doesn't yet know the agency's process. Judges clicking through in five minutes.

What changes from today

  • Sidebar client tabs become the rail (Home becomes the rail's summary header)
  • Research renamed Collect; Compare and Channel move inside Find with "Add to Collect"
  • Batch "Send N for approval" from stage 3
  • Make = checklist, Canvas, editor for one plan; Post is its own step
  • Demo asks "Who are you today?" and the avatar menu switches role

Trade-offs

  • + Easiest to learn, no dead ends by construction
  • + Batch actions fit naturally between stages
  • − Real work isn't linear: sent-back plans, re-picks and filming across days fight the rail
  • − Managers who only approve must walk through a client's rail; cross-client approval needs an extra page
  • − Six stages is a lot of chrome for a two-person agency

Flow 2 · hub and spoke

Client Hub

Two hubs. The agency hub is an inbox of what needs a person today across every client. The client hub is that client's Home: a card per question, each card a door to one focused page, and every page returns to the hub. People start from "what needs me", not from a search box.

Principle

Role-based landing. Managers land on an Approvals inbox across clients; team members land on "My work". Client Home is the centre, all other client pages are spokes with "Back to client home".

Serves best

Managers and account leads running many clients, and returning users who come back to finish things rather than start them.

What changes from today

  • New agency pages: Inbox (approvals of plans and cuts, all clients), My work, Clients, Agency settings (team and roles)
  • Picking a client opens Home, not Find ideas (reverses the 2 Nov decision)
  • Brief and memory become a Home card
  • Compare and Channel become agency tools with "Save to a client"

Trade-offs

  • + Fastest manager loop; approvals no longer need a client switch
  • + Fixes Brief, Compare and Channel dead ends with explicit returns
  • − One extra click to search, the most frequent team action
  • − Hubs need good data to feel useful; empty states matter more
  • − Reverses a founder decision (client opens Find ideas)

Flow 3 · board-centric

Video Board

The client's month is a board of the videos they pay for. Twelve videos a month means twelve cards. A card moves left to right: Needs a reference, Planning, Waiting, Approved, Making, Posted. Everything about one video (its references, plan, takes, cut and post) opens in that card. Find ideas becomes a panel that fills a card.

Principle

The unit of work is the paid video, not the search result. Research slots, plans and production stages already exist in the data; this flow puts them on one surface and removes Research and Plans as separate lists.

Serves best

Production-heavy teams who live in the work all week (planners, editors) and anyone who thinks "where's Video 7?". Also the client view later: the same board, read-only.

What changes from today

  • Plans board grows to six columns and takes Research's slots as its cards
  • Card workspace with tabs: References, Plan, Make, Post, Activity
  • Find ideas opens as a side panel scoped to a card ("Add to Video 3")
  • Canvas picker and sidebar Canvas go away; Canvas is the Make tab
  • Review = board filtered to "Waiting on me", then focus mode

Trade-offs

  • + One place per video, no back-stack confusion, no duplicate lists
  • + Cuts and plans share the same approval column
  • + The month target is literally the number of cards
  • − Exploring with no video in mind needs a home: a standing "Inbox ideas" card or Unsorted lane
  • − Dense board on small screens; needs a list view
  • − Biggest rebuild of the three

Recommendation: Flow 3, Video Board, with two borrowings

Build the Video Board as the client workspace. The data already points there: Research is grouped by the client's paid videos, a plan belongs to one of those videos, and approved plans already carry Filming, Editing and Posted stages. Putting them on one board removes the two biggest problems today: the same work split across Research, Plans, the Plan page, Canvas and the editor, and Back buttons that lose your place.

  • From Flow 2, take the manager's Inbox. One cross-client "Waiting on me" view with focus review, covering plans and finished cuts. It fixes gaps 1, 2 and 5.
  • From Flow 1, take the first-run guide and the role picker. "Who are you today?" on Try the demo, and a three-step guide on the first board, so the demo can show approval.
  • Keep the founder rule in spirit: opening a client lands on its board with the Find panel one click away ("+ Find references"), and a returning team member can keep Find as their default.

Do first, whichever flow wins (cheap, fixes real dead ends): role picker on Try the demo; Canvas and editor Back return to origin; finished cuts appear in Review; Compare and Channel results get "Save to Research"; a Brief link on Home; a Sent back lane on Plans; real URLs per screen so Back and refresh work.

This changes a major product decision (Research and Plans as separate tabs, and the sidebar from app-flow.md). Per CLAUDE.md, confirm with the founder before anyone builds it; nothing here touches src/.
