Unit

One pixel, one thing that counts. A calm, modern interface where a pixel only appears when Remake is counting something real: one square per video the client pays for, per second of a shot, per account posting a trend, per "usual" view count. Everything that is not a count stays plain Geist on white and beige. Mustard marks the one thing that needs you.
