    How it moves

    • On load: the reel spins fast past a few steps, slows down, and lands on 01.
    • After that: one step at a time. Each step rests for about 2 seconds, then rolls up in 0.45s with a small settle at the end, like a slot reel.
    • The mustard lines mark the payline. The step above and below show faintly, so it reads as a reel.
    • The counter (01 / 08 plus ticks) makes it read as one flow, start to finish.
    • The reel is as wide as the longest step, so "We help you" never moves.
    • Pause stops the reel. People who turn off motion on their device see step 01 with no spinning.
    • On phones "We help you" sits above the reel. Resize the window to see it.
