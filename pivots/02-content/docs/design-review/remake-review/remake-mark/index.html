Remake · brand mark proposal · 4 Nov 2026

One animation, three new marks.

The Render hero animation is kept exactly as it is: each tile is one video Remake read. They fly in from scattered positions, settle into a shape, and three of them turn mustard, the videos far above their usual views. Only the shape the tiles form changes. The current play mark comes first as the reference.

00Reference, today

Play mark

The current hero, from the Daisy Chain idea. It says "video" clearly, but every video app owns a play triangle. It says what Remake looks at, not what Remake does.

01Recommended

The Loop

A video goes round once and comes back as a new one.

"Re" is a turn: an arrow that comes back to where it started, rebuilt. It also grows straight out of the pixel ring already in the Render logo and the client's month ring, so the mark, the logo and the main progress UI become one family.

02Proposal

Original + Remake

Two vertical frames: the trend you found, and the one you'll film.

The outlined frame behind is the original video, only read. The solid frame in front is the client's version, planned and filled in. Both are 9:16, the shape of every short video, so it says "short-form" without a platform logo.

03Proposal

R-play monogram

A capital R whose counter is a play button.

It keeps what works in today's play mark (it reads as video) but makes it ours: the R is for Remake, and the play triangle is the hole in its bowl rather than a shape standing alone. The R reads first; the play is the second look. The most "ownable" of the three as an app icon.

Recommendation

Go with The Loop.

  1. It says the verb, not the medium. Play and frames say "video". The loop says "make it again", which is the product: many videos read, one plan made.
  2. It joins what already exists. The Render logo is a 7 × 7 pixel ring with one mustard segment, and the client's month is a ring that closes. The Loop is that ring with an arrowhead, so logo, hero and progress UI share one shape.
  3. It survives small. A thick ring and one arrowhead still read at 32px. The R-play needs 64px for its cut-out to show, and Original + Remake turns into two blocks at 32px.

Runner-up: R-play monogram, if the founders want a letter they can own on an app store and in a social avatar. Avoid the frames for the icon; they work better as an illustration inside the product.

How this was made: the tile engine is copied from Concept C's thumbs() (16px grid, 14px and 10px tiles with a little person glyph, 60 loose 4–8px squares, three mustard hits, 1.8s cubic ease-out from random positions, same seed). Only the shape test changed. With reduced motion turned on, each mark is drawn in its final state.
