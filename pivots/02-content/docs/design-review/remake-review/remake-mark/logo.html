Remake · logo sheet · proposal · 4 Nov 2026

The R as Remake's logo.

A pixel R whose counter is a play triangle. The static icon is the logo: whole pixels, one colour, no glyphs, no scatter. The tile animation is kept for a few brand moments only. The Loop runs through the same tests at the end, for comparison.

01

Static icon

Drawn on a 32 × 32 pixel grid as SVG rects with crisp edges. Every size shown is a whole-number multiple of its grid, so no pixel ever lands on a half pixel. 16px and 24px are hand-drawn, not shrunk: halving the 32 grid would turn the 9-row play triangle into 4½ rows, and 24 is not a whole multiple of 32.

The three grids, magnified ×8 (16 and 24) and ×6 (32), with the play counter in mustard so you can see it. In the logo it is an empty hole.

02

One-colour versions

The R is always one solid colour on one ground; the play triangle is a hole that shows the ground. A two-tone version fills the hole with mustard, the colour Remake already uses for "far above usual views". It earns its place on the app icon and avatar, where the play reads as a lit button, but stays out of the favicon and anywhere the logo is printed in one ink.

03

Lockups

Icon plus the lowercase remake wordmark in Inter Semibold, −2% tracking: the same style as the landing page's .rk-logo text. Note: the live landing currently shows a serif "Remake" image (remake-logo.png); adopting this sheet means swapping that image too.

Clear space

Keep a margin of one stem width on every side (the R's stem is 6 of its 32 pixels, so 3/16 of the icon height). The mustard squares show it. The gap between icon and wordmark is also ¾ of a stem.

Minimum size

Icon: 16px on screen, using the hand-drawn 16 grid. Below that, don't use it.
Horizontal lockup: icon 16px with the wordmark at 16px (about 80px wide).
Stacked lockup: icon 32px; below that use the horizontal one.
Print: 6mm icon, only at sizes where each pixel is at least 0.2mm.
Sizes: use 16, 24, 32 or a multiple of 32 so pixels stay whole. If a platform forces another size (the 180px Apple touch icon), put the 32 grid at ×5 = 160px on a 180px tile.

04

The animated mark

For brand moments only: landing hero, log in, searching, approved

The Render tile animation, unchanged, now forming the R from the same 32-pixel drawing as the static icon. Each tile is one video read; three turn mustard. Never use it as the logo in a header, favicon or avatar; those always get the static icon.

06

R versus The Loop

Same tests, same grids, same colours.

R-play (main)

The Loop

Verdict

Adopt the R. Keep the Loop as a motion idea, not a logo.

The R holds up at every test: it is legible at 16px, it is ours (a letter, not a generic symbol), and the play counter gives it a second read once you know it's there. The Loop is a strong story but a weak logo: at 16px it is the browser's reload button, and it sits next to the real one in every tab bar.

Before adopting it

  • Trademark search (a search, not legal clearance): "Remake" word mark plus pixel-R device marks in classes 9, 35 and 42, on IP Australia, WIPO Global Brand Database and USPTO. R monograms are a crowded space; a lawyer should clear it before money is spent.
  • Real devices: favicon in Chrome, Safari and Firefox tab bars in light and dark, on a non-retina screen; home screen icon on an iPhone and on an Android phone with a circle mask.
  • Platform exports: favicon.ico (16/32/48), apple-touch-icon 180 (32 grid ×5 on a 180 tile), Android adaptive icon (keep the R inside the 66% safe zone), 512 store icon, social avatars at 320 and 1080.
  • Wordmark decision: replace the serif "Remake" image on the landing page with the Inter lowercase lockup, or keep the serif and pair it with the R. Pick one.
  • Accessibility: contrast checks for each one-colour pairing in real UI (mustard on navy and blue on white pass for a graphic; check the wordmark text sizes).
  • Animation rules: confirm the four brand moments, the reduced-motion fallback (static icon), and that it never runs in loops longer than one play.
  • Master files: a Figma master (the 16, 24 and 32 grids as editable frames) and exported SVGs, so nobody redraws it by eye.
  • Handles and domain: check social handles and the domain match the lockup.
  • Founder sign-off on the R, the two-tone app icon, and the wordmark.
