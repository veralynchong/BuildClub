Foundation / Space

Shared edges. Predictable rhythm.

One 4px spacing scale, with fixed roles. Page styles choose the layout; they do not redefine the spacing values.

  • Page containerMaximum content width 1200px. Desktop padding 40px; tablet 24px; phone 20px.
  • Cards and forms24px padding and 24px gaps. Card radius 12px; input radius 8px.
  • Sidebar24px outer padding, 4px between nav rows, 24px between workflow sections.
  • Shared backgroundThe same 20px dot grid on Dashboard, Find ideas, Research and Plans. Cards and tables sit on solid white.
  • Responsive behaviourCards stack when their content no longer fits. Wide Research tables scroll with a visible edge. Controls wrap without shrinking the text.

Component rules

  • Buttons are actionsUse primary for the main next action, outline for supporting actions. No extra filled button colours.
  • Selections are statesSelected tabs and rows use a persistent 20% sky tint. They are not a third button style.
  • Picked is informationKeep the mustard “Picked” badge beside an outlined action; do not turn the action mustard.
  • Focus and motion2px sky focus outline with a 3px gap. Colour transitions only; reduced motion respected.
