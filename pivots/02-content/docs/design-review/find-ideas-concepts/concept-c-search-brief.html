Search brief: four choices, then results

Find ideas becomes a short, pre-filled form in the Plans page’s blueprint style: topic, where, who posted it, when. Saved briefs become the team’s shared research list for each client, re-checked daily.

In short
New user

A pre-filled brief teaches the method

Four blocks, already filled from the client brief. The account-size block makes the “small account, big views” rule a visible choice, with a suggested answer.

Returning user

Saved briefs, with new counts

A numbered list of the team’s briefs for this client, each re-checked daily. The brief stays beside the results as the filter panel.

Trade-offs

Clear and consistent, but slower

  • Feels like the same product as Plans
  • More clicks before the first result than a search box
  • Experienced users may skip it; needs the quick search row
