This week: a feed that’s ready when you open it

Find ideas opens on a weekly feed for the client, built from the brief and checked once a day. Search moves to a slim bar at the top. The question changes from “what should I search?” to “what’s new for Evergreen this week?”.

In short
New user

The brief builds the first feed

Three topics from the brief, checked once while you watch (about a minute). After that, a full page of ranked videos with no typing.

Returning user

“14 new since Thursday”

New videos first, split by topic. One-off searches still work, and a good search can become a weekly topic.

Trade-offs

Fastest feel, most to build

  • Needs a daily background check per client and topic (cost per search × clients)
  • Feeds can go stale or repetitive; topic quality decides everything
  • Biggest change from today’s screen
Journey 1 · New user

The “Leave out” line reuses the brief’s “can’t film” list, so restaurant videos never show up.
