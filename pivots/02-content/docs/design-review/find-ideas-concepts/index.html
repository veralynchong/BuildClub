Recommendation
Build next

Concept A, with two parts borrowed from B and C

A is the smallest step from today’s screen and covers both journeys well. It keeps search as the main action, which matches how Daphene’s team works today: search a keyword, look for small accounts with big views, shortlist. daphene-summary: 1-to-10 research

  1. Ship A’s return state first. Recent searches per client with “new since” counts, and saved videos beside them. This is the biggest gap today: a returning user sees the same empty page as a new one.
  2. Borrow C’s “Who posted it” as a visible default. Show “Under 50K followers” as a pre-set filter chip on results, so the agency’s rule is visible and one click to remove.
  3. Borrow B’s idea later, cheaply. Let a recent search be “watched”: the daily re-check runs only on searches the team chose. That is B’s feed without the cost of checking everything.
Why not B or C first
  • B feels fastest but is the most to build and the most expensive to run: a live search per topic, per platform, per client, every day. Prove people come back weekly with A’s “new since” counts before paying for a full feed.
  • C is the best match for the Plans look and teaches the method, but adds clicks before every search. Keep it in mind for onboarding new staff, or as the “Add client” step that writes the first starting searches.
Open questions for the founder
  1. Who pays for the daily re-check of recent searches, and is it limited (e.g. the last 5 searches per client)?
  2. Are recent searches shared by the whole team for a client, or private to each person?
  3. Should “Under 50K followers” be on by default? It hides big creators the client may still want.
  4. Which platforms are live? The landing page FAQ says “TikTok now, Instagram soon”, but the brief and the app’s toggles list TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and Reddit.
  5. Where do the brief-based starting searches come from: written by the team in Brief, or generated from it? If generated, who checks them?
