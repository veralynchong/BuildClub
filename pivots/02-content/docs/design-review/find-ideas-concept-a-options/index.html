Concept A, three ways to show your searches and saves

The founder picked Concept A (Search desk) but not the way it shows Recent searches and Saved to Research. These three options keep A’s flow and redesign those two areas, and the page around them, in the language of the approved Plans page and the built landing page.

00. What we kept, what we changed
Kept from Concept A

The same flow

  • New user: searches written from the brief, ideas catching on now, and question / link / screenshot inputs
  • Typing suggestions, then Best to copy and the grid
  • Returning user: recent searches for this client, re-checked daily, with “N new since Thu”, next to what is already saved
  • Filters as removable chips, with “Under 50K followers” on by default and labelled as such
  • Tiles: followers → views, title, account, link, + Save. Numbers in ink, “followers / views” and account in brand blue
What was wrong in A’s return state

Two cramped panels that didn’t say much

  • Recent searches and Saved sat side by side in two generic boxes, with mixed controls (pills, buttons, dashes) on every row
  • Saved to Research was a flat list of titles. It didn’t show which Research video each one is for, or what still needs a reference
  • The look didn’t match Plans (no tabs, numbers or ruled rows) or the landing (no section rhythm)
  • New in all three options: saved videos are grouped by the Research video they are for (Video 1, Video 2, Unsorted), the same “Which video is this for?” question the app already asks when you save
The three options

Ledger, Showcase, Desk rail

Each option page shows the same three states as full app frames: returning user, new user, and results with filter chips and saved feedback.

01. Rationale
Option 1 · Ledger

Read it like the shot list

Recent searches and Saved to Research are two numbered blueprint blocks, each a serif title on the left and ruled rows on the right, the exact structure of Plans. Rows line up in columns (new count, saved count), so you scan down one column instead of reading every row. Saved is listed by Research video with due date, mini covers, the best followers → views and “Needs a reference” for empty ones.

Option 2 · Showcase

See it like the landing

The landing’s navy dot hero and section rhythm (mono label, serif title with one italic word, content on the right). Recent searches are cards with a strip of covers, new ones marked with a mustard dot. Saved is a shelf of posters grouped by Research video. The most visual and the most “brand”, but covers take space, so fewer items fit.

Option 3 · Desk rail

Keep it beside you

A right rail with 01. Searches and 02. Saved to Research as tabbed blocks, on every Find ideas screen. The middle column shows the new videos themselves. Saving lands visibly in the rail, so you never save the same video twice. The rail costs a grid column and needs a separate phone layout.

03. Recommendation
Build

Option 1, the Ledger

It answers the two returning-user questions fastest, it is the same visual system as the Plans page the founder already approved, and it is the cheapest to build on today’s Find page.

  1. Build the two blocks (01. Recent searches, 02. Saved to Research) and the “Where to start” block for new users.
  2. Group saved videos by Research video, with “Needs a reference” on empty ones. This is the biggest gain in every option.
  3. Ship the default “Under 50K followers” chip with its “default” label.
Borrow

One idea each from 2 and 3

  • From Option 3: on the results page, the saved strip stays at the bottom (Option 1 already has it as a tabbed tray). If testing shows people still save twice, upgrade that strip to the rail later.
  • From Option 2: use the navy Plans stage for the Best to copy card, so the strongest pick looks like the video it will become on Plans.
  • Later: “watched” searches (the overview’s recommendation) fit Option 1 as a column on the ledger: a small toggle per row.
04. Open questions for the founder
  1. Group saved by Research video? All three options assume saved items show under Video 1, Video 2, Unsorted (the slots “Which video is this for?” already uses). Is that how the team thinks, or do they want a flat “recently saved” list?
  2. Whose searches? The rows show who ran each search (Sam, Priya). Should Recent searches be the whole team’s for this client, or only mine?
  3. How many recent searches? We show 4 with “All 7 searches”. Is the daily free re-check for every search or only the last few, and do old ones (over 7 days) stop being re-checked?
  4. Should “Needs a reference” suggest a search? Option 1 writes a suggested search from the Research video’s title. Useful, or noise?
  5. Is “Under 50K followers” the right default for every client, or set per client in the brief?
  6. Best to copy on navy? Do you want the Plans navy stage on Find ideas (Option 2), or keep navy for Plans only?
