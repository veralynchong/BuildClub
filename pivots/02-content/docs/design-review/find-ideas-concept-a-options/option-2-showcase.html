Option 2 · Showcase

The landing page, applied to Find ideas. A navy dot hero holds the search, then white sections in the landing rhythm: a mono label and a big serif title with one italic word on the left, the content on the right. Recent searches and Saved to Research become things you can see: cover strips and posters.

01 Returning·02 New user·03 Results + saved
01. Returning user

Searches as cards, saved as a poster shelf

Recent searches are four cards, each with a strip of covers. Covers with a mustard dot are new; “+6” says how many more. It reads at a glance which search has something worth opening.

Saved to Research is a shelf of posters grouped by the video they are for (Video 1, Video 2, Unsorted), with followers → views, title and account in blue. An empty Video 3 shows a dashed “Needs a reference” card.
