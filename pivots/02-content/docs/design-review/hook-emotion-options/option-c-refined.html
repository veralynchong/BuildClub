Option C, refined: one emotion list, one colour each

The founder picked Option C. This page fixes its two risks (the tag read as a button, and every emotion was blue) and merges the two emotion lists into one.

Recommendation

Tinted label, eight muted hues, eight emotions

  • Treatment: the tinted label. Square corners, mono caps, same-hue text on a pale tint. Every visual cue a Remake button has (pill, Inter, sentence case, blue) is reversed, and there is no hover, pointer or focus.
  • Palette: no blue at all. Teal, apricot, sage, plum, honey, rose, slate, brick. All are muted, none is near the primary blue #1a33f2, and every tag passes AA (6.3:1 to 7.8:1 light, 7.7:1 to 9.1:1 dark).
  • The word always shows. Colour helps scanning; the word carries the meaning. Screen readers hear “Viewer feels: Curiosity”.
  • One list of 8 replaces both the types.ts list and the proposed one (table below). The tofu hooks become Curiosity (teal), Relief (sage), FOMO / insider (plum).

Watch: Alarm (brick) and Surprise (apricot) are the closest pair; they differ in hue (red vs orange) and the word disambiguates. Honey and the “Best to copy” mustard are related but honey is only ever a pale tint, never the solid winner fill.

One emotion list

Eight emotions, named for what the viewer feels in the first two seconds. Each old value maps to exactly one new one.

EmotionThe viewer thinksReplaces (types.ts)Proposed listHue
Viewer feels: Curiosity“What happens next?”curiousCuriosityTeal
Viewer feels: Surprise“Wait, what?”excitedSurpriseApricot
Viewer feels: Relief“Finally, an easy way.”—ReliefSage
Viewer feels: FOMO / insider“Others know this and I don’t.”—FOMO / insiderPlum
Viewer feels: Humour“Ha. I’m sending this to someone.”funnyHumourHoney
Viewer feels: Desire“I want that.”cravingAspirationRose
Viewer feels: Recognition“That’s so me.”relatable—Slate
Viewer feels: Alarm“Wait, that’s wrong (or risky).”angry_commentAlarmBrick
  • Merges: craving and Aspiration become Desire (“I want that”, food or lifestyle); excited becomes Surprise; angry_comment folds into Alarm (the viewer feels something is wrong and wants to say so).
  • Loosest mappings: excited → Surprise and angry_comment → Alarm. Worth a quick check against the saved breakdowns before migrating.
  • For the build: suggested keys curiosity, surprise, relief, insider, humour, desire, recognition, alarm; the drawer’s .tag-emotion chip should switch to the same tag so the two screens agree.
